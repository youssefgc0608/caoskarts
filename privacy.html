Privacy policy

Caos Karts · effective 24 October 2026

In short: Caos Karts does not ask for your name, email or location, and has no user accounts. Your progress is saved only on your device. The game is made for all ages, including children.

1. Who is responsible

Caos Karts is run by Youssef Ghosn Cheri, based in Chile. You can write to youssefplay04@gmail.com with any question about this policy or your data.

2. What is stored on your device

The game stores in your browser or app storage: your progress (tokens, experience, stars, campaign, garage, shop, achievements, challenges, records and time-trial ghosts), your settings (volume, language, controls, graphics, accessibility) and a log of game errors. None of this is sent anywhere. If you clear the app or browser data, everything is deleted.

You can export your save or the error log from Settings. That file only leaves your device if you choose to share it.

3. What leaves your device

Today, nothing: the game works offline and makes no connections. When the following features are switched on (Android app only), this will apply:

Reward ads, only if you tap “watch ad”Technical device and ad data (model, system, approximate IP address, whether the ad was watched to the end). All ads are requested as child-directed and non-personalised: they are not used to build a profile of you.Google (AdMob)

We do not sell data, there are no ads during races or when the game opens, and no analytics that identify people are used. If an anonymous usage statistic is added in the future, it will be off by default, can be refused in Settings, and will be described here before it is switched on.

4. Children

Anyone can play Caos Karts. That is why we ask nobody for personal data, ads are always requested as child-directed and non-personalised, and there is an adult check before any real-money purchase. If you are a parent or guardian and have any question, write to the email in section 1.

5. Your rights

Because we keep none of your data on our servers, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete: you control it from your device. For data processed by Google or RevenueCat, you can exercise your rights with them; see their privacy policies. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and, for important changes, you will be told inside the game.
