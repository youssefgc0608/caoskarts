Privacy policy

Caos Karts · effective 24 October 2026 · updated 21 November 2026

In short: Caos Karts does not ask for your real name, email or location, and has no password accounts. Your progress is saved on your device. To play online, the game uses a random number that does not say who you are. The game is made for all ages, including children.

1. Who is responsible

Caos Karts is run by Youssef Ghosn Cheri, based in Chile. You can write to youssefplay04@gmail.com with any question about this policy or your data.

2. What is stored on your device

The game stores in your browser or app storage: your progress (tokens, experience, stars, campaign, garage, shop, achievements, challenges, records and time-trial ghosts), your settings (volume, language, controls, graphics, accessibility) and a log of game errors. None of this is sent anywhere. If you clear the app or browser data, everything is deleted.

You can export your save or the error log from Settings. That file only leaves your device if you choose to share it.

Races, the campaign and the Arena against the computer work offline. Only when you use an online feature, or in the Android app for the features below, this leaves your device:

We do not sell data, there are no ads during races or when the game opens, and no analytics that identify people are used. Game reminders (for example, “your daily reward is waiting”) are scheduled on your own device and go through no server; you can turn them off in your phone settings.

4. Children

Anyone can play Caos Karts. That is why we ask nobody for personal data, ads are always requested as child-directed and non-personalised, and there is an adult check before any real-money purchase. If you are a parent or guardian and have any question, write to the email in section 1.

5. Your rights

You control your save from your device. Our server only holds what section 3 lists, tied to a random number and not to you as a person. You can drop your name or leave your club inside the game at any time, and to see or delete the rest, write to the email in section 1 with your friend code (in “My account”). For data processed by Google or RevenueCat, you can exercise your rights with them; see their privacy policies. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country.

6. Videos on YouTube (YouTube API Services)

Caos Studio uses the YouTube API Services through an internal tool that uploads gameplay videos to our own YouTube channel, Don Caos Games, and reads their statistics (views, likes and comments). No one else uses this tool, and it does not access data from other channels or from players.

By using YouTube content you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Data and deletion: we only store, on our own computer, the IDs and statistics of our own videos, for at most 14 days; after that they are deleted. The permission granted to the tool can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page; once revoked, we delete stored data within 7 days. To request deletion sooner, email youssefplay04@gmail.com.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and, for important changes, you will be told inside the game.
